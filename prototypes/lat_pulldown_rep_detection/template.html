Lat pulldown Rep detection: prototype

Throwaway prototype for the ticket "Lat pulldown Rep detection". Data: MediaPipe Pose (the app's lite model) run over the 16 reference clips.

The question

How should the app tell that one lat pulldown Rep has happened, using only the body landmarks the camera gives it, without counting twice, missing a Rep, or counting someone who is just sitting there?

The idea being tested: watch how high the wrists are above the shoulder (measured in torso lengths, so it doesn't depend on how far the phone is). A Rep is counted when the arms go from up (above the top line), down to pulled (below the bottom line), and back up again. The gap between the two lines stops small wobbles from being counted.
